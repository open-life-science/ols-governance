# OLS - Non-Executive Directors (draft)

**Essential Requirements**

1. Uphold and apply OLS’s values, supporting the advancement of OLS’s vision and mission.
2. Advise the Executive Directors and team to help achieve the organisational strategy.
3. Contribute to promotional activities and fundraising, partnership development and community activities to support the Executive Directors and staff.
4. Constructively challenge the decision-making process to make processes consistent and transparent.
5. Operate at all times in the best interest of the nonprofit, including promptly disclosing potential or actual conflicts of interest, and comply with all applicable laws and obligations, including the Code of Conduct, Governance Guidelines, or any other relevant policies and procedures, including privacy and data protection requirements and guidelines.
6. Channel all official communication with staff and volunteers through the Executive Director, or conduct any business with the Executive Director’s direct request and express consent.
    1. This requirement should be described for each Non-Executive Director.

**Individual Duties and Responsibilities:**

* Attend and actively participate in dedicated meetings for Non-Executive Directors and other communication channels for ongoing discussions related to the OLS leadership without affecting the day-to-day operations led by the Executive Directors.
* Collaborate with the Executive Directors to drive strategic direction and define strategy and goals aligned with the OLS’s values and mission.
* Support the Executive Directors by managing expectations consistent with OLS's strategic priorities and ensuring adequate resources are available for staff to implement strategy and goals.
* Represent OLS by attending open science events on its behalf, contributing to communications efforts through online presence, and participating in community discussions while upholding the responsibilities outlined in this document.
* Although not involved in the day-to-day running of the business, they may assume other specific roles defined and agreed with the Executive Directors based on availability, subject-matter expertise and/or organisational needs.
* Contribute to the annual review of the organisational operations and financial planning, budgeting, and reporting processes.
* Provide information by established deadlines to enable Executive Directors to make timely decisions in the best interest of OLS.

**Expectations**

* Foster collaborative discussion and practice open communication to ensure awareness and transparency within OLS’s strategy and operations. Critique ideas constructively rather than criticising individuals, and address concerns about individuals directly with them first, whenever possible.
* While disagreements are natural in decision-making and deliberation, publicly support all decisions, particularly those that do not achieve unanimous consent.
* Engage in leadership discussions by exploring issues within the context of the organisation's values, missions and goals.
* Expect and encourage Executive Directors and staff to respond straightforwardly and thoroughly to questions, enabling directors to fulfil their legal, operational and other responsibilities to the organisation.
* Aim to remediate mistakes quickly, honestly, and thoroughly. Recognising that members are human and will make mistakes, they are expected to act swiftly and fully to acknowledge and correct them.
* Support Executive Directors and staff members in their ambassadorial roles, especially where requested.

**Resource currently required to fulfil duties**

* Executive Coaching (budget may be required, for example, for BetterUp)
* Budget to attend OLS staff retreat and other events where requested to attend or represent the organisation
* Access to the OLS infrastructure, required to fulfil OLS duties and responsibilities
* Clear process for maintaining involvement as agreed with the Executive Directors

**How is this role different from the Governance Advisory Committee in OLS?**

The Non-Executive Director roles are assumed by co-founders or previous Executive Directors. Their involvement should help maintain** institutional memory, embedding a deep understanding of long-term vision and sharing firsthand experience** with the operational and strategic challenges faced during the organisation's development. Their primary function is to provide advice, support and strategic guidance. They **may have direct involvement or leadership within defined areas where requested or approved by the Executive Directors**, ensuring that operations and decisions align with the core values and mission of OLS.

The OLS Governance Advisory Committee is comprised of individuals who are elected and appointed directly from the broader OLS community. The committee's primary role is to offer **transparency, accountability and advisory support** across all OLS operations, and to support the long-term sustainability of the organisation.[ OLS governance documentation](https://github.com/open-life-science/ols-governance) provides guidelines and a community-informed approach to ensuring governance improvement in OLS.

**Resigning from the Non-Executive Director Roles**

Non-Executive Directors may resign from their positions under various conditions, including personal reasons, health concerns, or a change in their professional commitments that prevents them from dedicating the necessary time and effort to their duties.

The roles of Non-Executive Directors can be evaluated every 6 months to adjust expectations and/or end the agreement if the position-holder is unable to fulfil their role. This is also an opportunity to update the role if the position holder is taking leadership or other responsibilities that require monetary compensation.

They may also choose to resign if they perceive a fundamental disagreement with the strategic direction or governance practices of the organisation, or if there are concerns about conflicts of interest that cannot be adequately managed.

Typically, a formal letter of resignation is submitted to the Executive Directors. A Non-Executive Director is expected to provide reasonable notice to allow for a smooth transition without losing institutional knowledge/memory.
